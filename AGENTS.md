## Hard anti-patterns (never do by default)

- Geo-IP or `Accept-Language` force redirects
- Changing locked third-party booking/profile URLs
- Emitting hreflang for noindex / 404 / placeholder pages
- Calquing English keyword maps into other locales
- Advertising two public sitemap entrypoints
